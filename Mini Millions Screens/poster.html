Mini Millions is now on your phone

See the night, post your score, and follow the field. When the round is over, the winners are right at the top.

Home

The whole season in one place

Tonight’s event is the big card at the top. Upcoming nights and finished rounds sit underneath. Switch seasons from the menu, and open Players to see the league.

During the round

Enter a score in a couple of taps

Open the hole, tap plus or minus, and move on. A birdie, par, or bogey shows under the number. The leaderboard ticker runs across the top while you play.

Your card

See the round, then fix a hole

Under par is a circle. Over par is a square. A gold dot marks a stroke. Tap any hole to jump back and change a score.

While it’s live

Follow the field

Names, photos, and the score to par update as groups post. A team of two shares one circle. Tap a row to open that card.

When it’s over

Winners, photos, and the pin

First and second get the trophies. Closest to the pin is on the same list, with a flag in the hole. The full leaderboard is one tab away.
